MirDB: The Keeper of Keys

In depths of Rust, where memory holds fast,
MirDB guards what time would have erased.
Each key and value, paired and bound to last,
Through layered halls where data finds its place.

First to the log, the witness writes in haste,
A promise kept before the ink has dried.
Then swift to mind, where skip lists interlaced
Hold thoughts that persist, never cast aside.

When memory fills, the cascade starts its course,
From active pools to still, immutable streams.
Down through the levels, ever-growing force,
Where sorted tablets store eternal dreams.

The compaction comes, a patient, tireless hand,
That merges scattered pages into one.
Distilling chaos into order planned,
Until the deepest level's work is done.

So trust your data to MirDB's care,
Where Rust-forged walls stand guard through endless night.
A persistent store beyond compare,
Transforming fleeting bytes to lasting light.
