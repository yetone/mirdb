MirDB: An Ode to Persistence

In memory's fleeting embrace, data once did dwell,
Lost to power's whim, a volatile spell.
But MirDB rises, forged in Rust's might,
A guardian of records through endless night.

Not like memcached, whose treasures fade away,
When servers sleep or circuits cease to play.
Here data persists, etched deep in disk,
No transient cache, no ephemeral risk.

Through LSM trees, your bytes cascade down,
From memtable heights to SSTable's crown.
The Write-Ahead Log stands sentinel true,
A promise keeper, durability's due.

What memory forgets, the disk remembers still,
Sorted strings preserved through crash and kill.
Compaction weaves through layers deep,
Eternal records that forever keep.

So trust your keys to MirDB's care,
Persistent storage beyond compare.
Where others vanish in power's failing light,
Your data endures, permanent and bright.
