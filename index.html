MirDB

Persistent Key-Value Store with Memcached Compatibility

MirDB combines the simplicity of memcached's protocol with the reliability of persistent storage. Built with Rust for safety and performance, MirDB uses an LSM-tree architecture to deliver fast writes while ensuring your data survives restarts. Drop-in replacement for memcached with data durability.
