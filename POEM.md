# MirDB: A Verse

In circuits deep where data streams,
A guardian stands of bytes and dreams,
MirDB awaits with steadfast grace,
A keeper of pairs in ordered space.

Each key unlocks a treasured store,
Each value safe forevermore,
From memory's swift and fleeting art
To disk where records never part.

Through layers deep the data flows,
From memtable's heights where knowledge grows,
Through sorted strings on tablets pressed,
In LSM trees the bits find rest.

Forged in Rust, unbreaking, true,
A sentinel in crimson hue,
Where keys and values intertwine,
Your data persists through endless time.

No cache that fades when power dies,
But steadfast truth that never lies,
A key-value store that stands alone,
MirDB guards what you have sown.
